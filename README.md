# 🚗 Electric Vehicle Market Intelligence Platform

An end-to-end data analytics and web integration project designed to transform global Electric Vehicle (EV) market data into actionable strategic insights. This project embeds interactive Tableau visualizations into a custom web application, making complex vehicle specs, sales trajectories, and technical metrics accessible to industry analysts, manufacturers, and buyers.

## 🎯 Project Objective
The goal of this project is to process and visualize global EV datasets to uncover patterns in vehicle adoption, regional sales distribution, pricing vs. driving range efficiency, and battery technology performance. By transitioning the analysis into an integrated dashboard and multi-scene Tableau Story layout, complex market findings become centralized and easy to present.

## 📊 Key Features & Analysis
- *Dynamic Executive KPI Cards:* High-level metrics tracking total manufacturers, EV models, total annual sales, average customer ratings, driving range, and battery capacity.
- *Category & Sales Breakdown:* Visual insights into sales distribution across vehicle segments (Scooters, Sedans, SUVs, and Trucks).
- *Pricing & Efficiency Analytics:* Scatter plot evaluation analyzing price-to-range ratios and horsepower benchmarks.
- *Geographic & Battery Insights:* Global map distribution paired with battery degradation matrix and chemistry market shares.
- *Interactive Narrative Story:* A structured 9-scene Tableau Story for guided executive walkthroughs and strategic presentations.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- *Data Processing:* Python / Pandas / Excel CSV Pipelines
